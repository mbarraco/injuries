A move is one row, however many sources reported it. A transfer between two clubs we cover is reported by both clubs and by the player. Those are collapsed into a single row — {{ '{:,}'.format(overview.confirmed_both) }} of the moves below carry corroboration from more than one source, which is a confidence signal, not extra volume.
