This vendor's coverage caveat is a different shape from Sportmonks', not a smaller version of the same one. Sportmonks' limit is a backfill ramp: older years genuinely have fewer records because the vendor added history gradually, so cross-year counts there measure backfill, not football. This vendor's limit is grain: every row is one player missing one fixture, with no spell identifier — a single injury spanning 8 matches is 8 rows here, and there is no way to collapse them back into "one injury" without guessing a gap threshold. {{ af.grain_note(overview.grain_note) }}

Known gaps

  • No spell identifier. Absence counts here are (player, fixture) appearances; they are not comparable row-for-row with Sportmonks' injury view, which counts whole spells.
  • `Missing Fixture` vs `Questionable` must never be summed. One is a confirmed absence, the other a doubt that may not have materialised — see the confirmed-vs-questionable split above.
  • Players injured for a whole season have no minutes denominator. They are still counted in every absence total; their rate is undefined, not zero.
  • Transfer history is NOT bounded by these seasons. Unlike every table above, af_transfer reaches back decades further than the vendor's fixture/injury coverage — see Transfers.
