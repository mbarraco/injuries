Injury coverage over time

Sidelined records per fixture, by year. The climb is the vendor backfilling history, not injuries becoming more frequent — so comparing injury counts, or rates, across years measures Sportmonks' coverage rather than football.

The series changes composition in 2024. Before that it is UEFA cups alone; from 2024 it is those cups plus 58 domestic leagues. The 2023→2024 step therefore blends a coverage change with a mix change and should not be attributed to either on its own.

Coverage by competition

Depth differs by competition by design: domestic leagues expose exactly 3 seasons (2024/25–2026/27), the hard ceiling of the subscription plan, while the UEFA cups reach back to 2000.

Known gaps

  • Injuries-focused views. The vendor combines injuries and suspensions in one feed; these views show injuries, but every category is now retained in the absence table (see the per-category counts above) rather than dropped at ingest.
  • Vendor inconsistency. The same concept arrives as suspended and suspension; both are measured and kept, not silently merged.
  • Missing dimensions are retained. Unresolvable teams and player IDs do not cause injury rows to be discarded.
  • Season attribution is derived. The vendor omits it from sideline records, so it comes from the parent fixture.
  • History depth is a plan ceiling, and it is settled. Domestic leagues expose exactly 3 seasons (2024/25–2026/27); the UEFA cups reach back to 2000. Sparse older coverage is what the subscription sells, not a gap another ingest run would fill.
