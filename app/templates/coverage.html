Known gaps

  • Suspensions excluded. The vendor combines injuries and suspension records in one feed; this POC keeps only injuries.
  • Vendor inconsistency. The same concept arrives as suspended and suspension; both are measured and excluded during ingest.
  • Missing dimensions are retained. Unresolvable teams and player IDs do not cause injury rows to be discarded.
  • Season attribution is derived. The vendor omits it from sideline records, so it comes from the parent fixture.
  • History depth is provisional. Sparse older coverage may reflect trial-account restrictions; it is not presented as a settled limitation.
